# Deleting your account

Deleting your account removes your profile and signs you out everywhere.

## Before you delete

If you own a workspace, transfer ownership to another member first, or delete the workspace. Export anything you want to keep, because deletion cannot be undone.

## Delete the account

Open Settings, then Account, and select "Delete account". Confirm with your password. We remove your personal data within 30 days. Invoices are kept for seven years because the law requires it.
