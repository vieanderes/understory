# Billing and invoices

Plans are billed monthly or yearly, per member.

## Change your plan

Owners can change plans under Settings, then Billing. Upgrades take effect straight away and we charge the difference for the rest of the billing period. Downgrades take effect at the next renewal date.

## Invoices

Every payment creates an invoice. Download invoices as PDF from Settings, Billing, Invoice history. To add a VAT number or change the billing address, edit the billing details before the next payment; past invoices cannot be changed.

## Refunds

Yearly plans cancelled within 14 days of payment are refunded in full. Monthly plans are not refunded, but you keep access until the end of the month you paid for.
