# Resetting your password

If you forget your password, you can set a new one from the sign-in page.

## Request a reset link

Select "Forgot password" on the sign-in page and enter the email address on your account. We send a reset link within a few minutes. The link works once and expires after 30 minutes. If it has expired, request a new one.

## Choose a new password

Passwords need at least 12 characters. You cannot reuse any of your last five passwords. After you save the new password, every other session is signed out.

## No email arrived

Check your spam folder first. Reset emails come from the no-reply address. If nothing arrives after 15 minutes, your account may use single sign-on, in which case your organisation's administrator manages your password.
