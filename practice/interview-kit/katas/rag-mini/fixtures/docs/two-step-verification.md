# Two-step verification

Two-step verification asks for a code from your phone as well as your password when you sign in.

## Turn it on

Open Settings, then Security, and select "Turn on two-step verification". Scan the QR code with an authenticator app and enter the six-digit code it shows. We also give you ten backup codes. Store them somewhere safe; each works once.

## Lost your phone

Sign in with one of your backup codes instead of the six-digit code. Then turn two-step verification off and on again to link your new phone. If you have no backup codes left, contact support with proof of identity. Recovery takes up to three working days.
